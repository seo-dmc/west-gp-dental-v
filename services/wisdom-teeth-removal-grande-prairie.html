Wisdom Teeth Removal Grande Prairie: Protect Your Oral Health

Wisdom teeth, also known as third molars, typically emerge in the late teens or early twenties. While some people's wisdom teeth develop without issues, many experience complications such as impaction, crowding, or infection. Wisdom Teeth Removal Grande Prairie at West Grande Prairie Dental is a common procedure designed to prevent these problems and protect the overall health and alignment of your smile. Our team of experts provides safe, comfortable, and efficient extractions for patients of all ages.

When wisdom teeth are "impacted," it means they don't have enough room to fully emerge or grow in correctly. This can cause them to grow at an angle, pushing against neighboring teeth and causing pain or misalignment. Impacted wisdom teeth can also be difficult to clean, making them more susceptible to decay and gum disease. Even if your wisdom teeth aren't causing pain yet, we often recommend removal as a preventive measure based on X-rays and clinical evaluations to avoid future dental crises.
